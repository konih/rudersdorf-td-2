## Decisions taken inside the task

- **`karten` stand-in.** `ersatz_lies_karte` never looks at the text; the map is the constant
  `FLUSSTAL` in `rudersdorf/ersatz.py` ("the built-in map" of the contract). It is not read from
  `karten/flusstal.txt`, because reading and splitting a file is mission 1. T006's test compares
  the constant with the file, so the two cannot drift apart unnoticed.
  `ersatz_finde` does not search: it answers `[(0, 7)]` for `"S"`, `[(17, 8)]` for `"Z"` and `[]`
  for anything else, whatever grid it is given. The engine (T015) must therefore not use `finde`
  for anything but the two gates.
- **`wegfindung` stand-in ("ghost walk").** `ersatz_abstandskarte`: Manhattan distance for every
  cell. `ersatz_ist_begehbar`: true for every cell of the grid. `ersatz_nachbarn`: right, left,
  below, above (not the mission's order), cells outside the grid left out, so the engine never
  gets an index outside the grid. `ersatz_naechster_schritt`: the first neighbour (in the
  stand-in's order) with a smaller distance, not the smallest. On `flusstal` the ghost reaches
  the village gate in 18 steps through the river; the reference route has 24.
- **`bauregel` stand-in.** False outside the grid, off `BEBAUBAR`, on a cell in `besetzt`, and
  within the eight cells around (or on) any cell in `gesperrt`; otherwise true. No route is
  searched: it allows sealing a gate that the mission forbids (row 9), which is what the
  engine's safety net is for. The terrain and border checks are in because FR-004's first
  clause should hold with mission 5 open as well; they are not the idea of mission 5.
- **Reference.** The border test is written out in `ist_begehbar` and again in `darf_bauen`
  instead of a helper: a learner compares function with function. `naechster_schritt` keeps the
  explicit "distance 0 → `None`" of the contract although the loop already implies it.
- **A new test module** (`tests/kern/test_ersatz_a_rand.py`) rather than additions to T006's
  file: T006's tests stay byte-identical. It fixes what T006 left "not asserted".
