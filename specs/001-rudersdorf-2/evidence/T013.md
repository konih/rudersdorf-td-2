## Decisions where plan and tests are silent

- **Swamp**: the speed of a stretch is decided by the cell walked into. So a step's length does
  not change where an enemy is (the laboratory's coarse step gives the same positions).
- **`rest_weg`** = distance of `ziel_zelle` times `ZELLE` plus the pixels to its centre (for an
  enemy on a centre: distance of `zelle` times `ZELLE`). It is re-measured for every enemy after
  each accepted building and each sale, so it is also right mid-step after a re-route.
- **`ziel_zelle`** is `None` while an enemy stands exactly on a cell centre (new, or arrived with
  no time left); `zelle` changes on arrival at the centre. `besetzt` holds both cells of every
  enemy. Until T031 every kind walks, flyers and breakers included (tasks.md, T015), and all are
  in `besetzt`; T031 changes movement and `besetzt` together.
- **`erzeuge_gegner(start=n)`**: `n` is the number of the orc gate in `karte.starts` (rows from
  the top); without `zelle` and `start` the first gate. The life factor is not applied here
  yet: T015 decides how wave and difficulty reach `Gegner(..., leben_faktor)`.
- **Order in `baue`**: phase, unknown kind, the rule (through `bauregel`), the engine's own look
  (cell on the map, buildable terrain, free, no enemy; then the trial distance map with every
  gate and every occupied cell), money, payment. The engine's look runs whatever the rule said:
  after a no it supplies the reason for `meldung` (the rule returns only a truth value), after a
  yes it is the safety net. The contract names only the route for the net; the cell checks were
  added because a rule that says yes to a cell outside the map would otherwise crash the match
  (Constitution II). The trial map of an accepted building becomes the map in use.
- **Messages**: a net refusal adds "Deine Bauregel hätte das verbieten müssen (Mission 5)." only
  when `bauregel` is solved. A no for which the engine sees no reason reads "Solange die Mission
  5 offen ist, muss rund um jedes Bauwerk eine Zelle frei bleiben." under the stand-in and "Deine
  Bauregel (Mission 5) erlaubt hier kein Bauwerk." otherwise. A success clears `meldung`.
- **Maps**: `Karte` finds the gates in the grid itself (the `karten` stand-in's `finde` knows
  only `flusstal`); `lade_karte` names the map `flusstal` while `karten` is open, because the
  stand-in returns that grid; an unreadable file is a `ValueError` naming the file.
  `lies_raster` turns a `Lernfehler` with `art == "ValueError"` into `ValueError`, any other
  `Lernfehler` passes (a code error is not a broken map).
- **`pruefe_karte`** checks the route with its own flood fill (no workbench there, T012); its
  docstring says that routes in the game come from the workshop.
- **Events**: `gebaut` `{zelle, bauwerk_art}`, `verkauft` `{zelle, bauwerk_art, gold}`,
  `durchbruch` `{schaden, gegner_art}`.
- **Phases**: `schritt`, `baue` and `verkaufe` act only in `bauen` and `welle`. A village life of
  0 does not end the match yet (T015: victory and defeat).
- **`Karte`** has `gesperrt`, `abstaende(werkbank)`, `abstaende_mit(gesperrt, werkbank)`,
  `sperre(zelle, abstaende=None)`, `gib_frei(zelle)`, `vergiss_abstaende()` (for the reload key,
  T017), `gelaende(zelle)`, `enthaelt(zelle)`. `Dorf` has `fehlende_vorraete(kosten)`.
- **`Bauwerk`**: `strategie` is `None` and `ausgegeben` 0 until the match sets them; `Gegner`
  keeps `faehigkeiten == []` (T015 builds them through the workbench).

## Learnings

Class: guidance (three lines in loop.md, *Lessons*; none needs a sensor or a tool).

- Fixed points that pass at the first run prove the fixed points only: list every decision
  taken beyond them, write one test each, aim the mutants at those lines.

  no and is the safety net after a yes.

  the word "never" against every early return.
